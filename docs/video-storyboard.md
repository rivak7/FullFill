## 2. Show and explain the problem

### Shots and action

1. Bird's-eye view of a person preparing an order.
2. The person ships the order out.
3. At the customer, show the food and a paper with the order written in large text side by side.
4. Make the discrepancy immediately visible to the audience.

- Narrate the customer-side comparison.
